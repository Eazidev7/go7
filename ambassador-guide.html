How it works — step by step

  1. You share your link. Customer taps it — the code is remembered automatically, or they can type it in at signup.
  2. They sign up + verify their phone. The customer is locked to you forever. First ambassador to reach them wins — no one can take them later, and they can't switch ambassadors.
  3. They place real orders. You earn on their first paid, delivered order — and keep earning on their repeat orders.
  4. You get paid weekly. Rewards are reviewed for fraud, approved, then sent to your bank account.

FAQ

Can I refer existing Go7 users? No — the credit and rewards are for acquiring NEW customers.

What if they don't enter my code? The link saves it automatically when they tap it — always share the link, and remind them to check the code field at signup.

Does their app-install journey matter? If they install from the Play Store first, they must enter your code manually at signup — remind them.

Why is my reward still PENDING? Every reward is reviewed before payout. Approved ones pay out in the weekly batch.

My reward was voided — why? Almost always: the order was cancelled/refunded, or the account failed fraud checks (duplicate phone, self-referral).

Is there a limit? The pilot has monthly campaign budgets — rewards are first-come. Push early in the month.

Can two ambassadors claim the same customer? No — the first valid attribution wins permanently.

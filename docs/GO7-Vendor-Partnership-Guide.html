1. Executive Summary

GO7 is an all-in-one delivery platform helping restaurants, food vendors, groceries, pharmacies, and local shops in Abeokuta, Nigeria reach more customers, take orders online, get paid securely, and have deliveries handled by GO7 riders.

For vendors, GO7 provides:
  • A digital storefront on the GO7 app and website
  • Real-time order management in your dashboard
  • Delivery & logistics — riders, live GPS tracking, PIN verification
  • Payments & wallet — Paystack, earnings ledger, bank withdrawals
  • Marketing tools — featured placement, Combo Rush flash deals

Commercial model: GO7 charges 8% commission on food/product sales only (not on delivery fees). Minimum withdrawal: ₦10,000.

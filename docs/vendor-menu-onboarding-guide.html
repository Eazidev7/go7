Vendor Menu Onboarding Guide

Pictorial handbook for onboarding agents — how vendors list food and how customers order rice, beans, egg, water, and more.

GO7 Nigeria · go7.ng · Version 1.0

1. Three building blocks

Main product

One plate or drink with a price. Example: Rice & Beans — ₦1,200.

Add-ons (extras)

Items that go with that plate. Example: Fried Egg +₦200, Water +₦150.

Separate products

Standalone items. Customer adds each alone: Rice, Beans, Egg, Water.

Agent tip: One plate = one main product. Things with that plate = add-ons. Things ordered alone = separate products.

2. Vendor dashboard steps

  1. Login at go7.ng/vendor/login → Dashboard → Products.
  2. Tap Add Product. Enter name, price (₦), description, category, photo.
  3. Under Extras for this dish, add Fried Egg, Water, Extra Portion if needed.
  4. Save. Menu goes live at go7.ng/vendor/[vendor-id].

5. FAQ

No "Add extras" button for customer?
Product has no add-ons — vendor must add extras and save.
Vendor not on homepage?
Must be admin-approved with at least one active product.
Water — add-on or separate?
With a meal → add-on. Sold alone often → separate product.
Why "Add extras" not "Customize"?
Clearer for Nigerian customers — optional items with a dish.

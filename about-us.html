About GO7

GO7 is a modern on-demand delivery and lifestyle platform designed to simplify how people move food, groceries, packages, and daily essentials across Nigerian cities. We provide a fast, reliable, and affordable way for customers to get what they want—delivered instantly.

Our Mission

To empower customers, vendors, and riders with a clean, intelligent, and stress-free delivery experience—built with transparency, speed, and technology at its core.
